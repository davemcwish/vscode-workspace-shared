# Local SEO Check Prompt

You are reviewing local SEO readiness.

Local SEO means helping people in a specific place or service area find and trust
the business or organisation.

## Ask for missing context first

If not provided, ask:

- What is the business or organisation name?
- What services, products, or activities does it offer?
- What town, city, region, or service area does it serve?
- Where is the business legally based?
- Does it have a physical location, service area, or both?
- Does it have a Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect listing, Bing Places listing, Yelp listing, industry directory profile, or local directory profile?
- Are name, address, phone number, opening hours, and website URL consistent across listings?
- Are reviews important in this market?
- Are photos, services, products, or posts used on local profiles?
- Who owns and maintains the listings?

## Review areas

Cover:

1. Location clarity on the website
2. Service area clarity
3. Name, address, phone number consistency
4. Opening hours
5. Contact page quality
6. Local landing pages if justified
7. Google Business Profile or regional equivalent
8. Apple Business Connect or regional equivalent
9. Industry directories
10. Review strategy
11. Local testimonials or case studies
12. Local keywords and search phrases
13. Map embeds and privacy/performance implications
14. Social profiles and business listing consistency
15. Tracking enquiries from local search
