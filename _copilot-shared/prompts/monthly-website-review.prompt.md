# Monthly Website Review Prompt

You are helping run a monthly website improvement review.

The goal is to keep the website accurate, useful, accessible, secure, findable,
measurable, trustworthy, and aligned with business or organisational goals.

This is a practical monthly review, not a full redesign exercise. Prefer small,
realistic improvements that a small team, beginner, or non-technical website
owner can complete.

**Currentness warning:** Website platforms, analytics tools, search tools,
privacy/cookie expectations, accessibility expectations, security practices,
social platforms, review platforms, and local listing features change over time.
Where current platform, legal, compliance, provider, or tool details matter, tell
the user what to verify from official sources.

---

## Monthly review principles

- Focus on evidence from the month, not guesses.
- Separate urgent fixes from useful improvements.
- Prefer small monthly improvements over unnecessary redesigns.
- Do not promise rankings, traffic, leads, sales, or conversion increases.
- Do not recommend fake reviews, fake locations, fake urgency, misleading
  claims, copied content, hidden text, or keyword stuffing.
- Do not ignore broken forms, failed bookings, payment issues, security issues,
  privacy risks, accessibility barriers, or incorrect business information.
- Mark missing data clearly instead of inventing results.
- If analytics, Search Console, conversion tracking, or local listing data is
  unavailable, recommend setting up beginner-safe measurement where appropriate.

---

## Ask for missing context first

If not provided, ask concise questions about:

- What type of website is this: public, internal, local business, brochure,
  eCommerce, booking, membership, content, static site, no-code site, CMS site,
  generated static report, or custom web application?
- What is the main goal of the website?
- What are the main conversion actions: enquiries, calls, bookings, purchases,
  donations, downloads, signups, applications, visits, support requests, or
  something else?
- What platform, CMS, website builder, hosting provider, or deployment method is
  used?
- Who maintains the website?
- Who owns the domain, hosting/platform, analytics, search tools, social
  profiles, and business listings?
- What changed this month on the website, in the business, or in the market?
- Are analytics available?
- Are conversions or key actions tracked?
- Are Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, or regional equivalents
  available?
- Are local listing, review, or business profile insights available?
- How many enquiries, bookings, signups, sales, donations, downloads, calls, or
  other conversions happened?
- Were there any support issues, form failures, booking failures, payment
  failures, email delivery issues, broken links, downtime, complaints, spam, or
  suspicious activity?
- Are there new services, products, prices, hours, locations, staff, events,
  policies, legal pages, or claims to update?
- Are there new reviews, testimonials, case studies, photos, awards,
  certifications, questions, objections, or customer feedback?
- Were any privacy, cookie, tracking, analytics, advertising, accessibility,
  security, payment, or platform changes made?
- What is the realistic time or budget available for improvements next month?

If information is unavailable, mark it as missing and recommend a simple way to
collect it next month.

---

## Review cycle

Use this improvement cycle:

```text
Launch
  → Measure
  → Learn
  → Improve
  → Maintain
  → Repeat
```

Do not skip the "Measure" and "Learn" steps. If there is not enough data, say
what data should be collected before making larger decisions.

## Review areas

Cover:

1. Website goal progress
2. Analytics and traffic quality
3. Enquiries, bookings, purchases, donations, downloads, calls, signups, or other
   conversions
4. Search visibility and SEO
5. Local SEO and business listings if relevant
6. Content freshness and accuracy
7. Product, service, price, opening hour, staff, location, event, policy, and
   legal-page updates
8. Broken links and broken journeys
9. Forms, booking links, payment links, downloads, maps, phone links, email
   links, and email delivery
10. Mobile usability
11. Accessibility basics
12. Performance and page speed
13. Security, backups, admin access, and suspicious activity
14. Privacy, cookie, consent, analytics, advertising, and tracking changes
15. Social media and online presence
16. Reviews, testimonials, case studies, and reputation
17. Third-party embeds, plugins, apps, integrations, and tracking pixels
18. Support issues, complaints, customer questions, and user feedback
19. Competitor or market observations where relevant
20. Next month's realistic priorities

## Monthly data checks

When data is available, review:

- total visits or users,
- top landing pages,
- top exit pages where useful,
- pages with low engagement or obvious problems,
- traffic sources,
- search queries,
- pages receiving search traffic,
- conversions by source where available,
- form submissions,
- calls,
- bookings,
- purchases,
- downloads,
- signups,
- campaign links,
- local profile clicks or calls where available,
- review volume and rating trends where relevant,
- errors, spam, downtime, or failed journeys.

When data is missing, do not invent numbers. State what is missing and suggest
how to collect it next month.

---

## Monthly improvement guidance

For each recommendation, explain:

- what was observed,
- why it matters,
- what to do next,
- who should own it,
- whether it is urgent, important, optional, or can wait,
- whether technical help is needed.

Prioritise:

1. broken critical journeys,
2. incorrect business or legal information,
3. security, privacy, accessibility, or payment risks,
4. high-value content updates,
5. SEO and local SEO improvements,
6. conversion improvements,
7. performance improvements,
8. nice-to-have design or content polish.

Do not recommend a redesign unless evidence shows the current site cannot meet
its goals with smaller fixes.
