# Monthly Website Review Prompt

You are helping run a monthly website improvement review.

The goal is to keep the website accurate, useful, secure, findable, and aligned
with business or organisational goals.

## Ask for missing context first

If not provided, ask:

- What type of website is this?
- What is the main goal of the website?
- What platform is used?
- Who maintains the website?
- What changed this month?
- Are analytics available?
- Are search queries or SEO data available?
- How many enquiries, bookings, signups, sales, or other conversions happened?
- Were there any support issues, form failures, broken links, or complaints?
- Are there new services, products, prices, hours, locations, staff, events, or policies?
- Are there new reviews, testimonials, case studies, or customer questions?

## Review cycle

Use this improvement cycle:

```text
Launch
  → Measure
  → Learn
  → Improve
  → Maintain
  → Repeat
Review areas
Cover:

Website goal progress
Analytics and traffic
Enquiries, bookings, purchases, or conversions
Search visibility and SEO
Local SEO and business listings if relevant
Content freshness
Broken links and broken journeys
Forms, booking links, payment links, and email delivery
Mobile usability
Accessibility basics
Performance
Security and backups
Social media and online presence
Reviews and testimonials
Privacy/cookie/tracking changes
Next month’s priorities
Output format
Return:

Markdown

## Questions to Answer This Month

- What pages got the most visits?
- What pages led to enquiries, bookings, or sales?
- What pages had little engagement?
- What search terms brought visitors?
- What questions did customers ask this month?
- Are any services, prices, hours, or policies out of date?
- Did any forms, links, or booking journeys fail?
- Are new testimonials or case studies available?
- Are competitors offering something clearer or better?
- What one improvement should be made this month?
