Help me plan a website from the initial idea through live launch and ongoing
maintenance.

Assume I may have little or no experience with websites, HTML, CSS, domains,
hosting, CMS platforms, accessibility, privacy, cookies, security, search engine
optimization, analytics, social media, online reviews, local SEO, forms,
bookings, payments, or digital marketing.

Do not start with code.
Do not start by choosing a platform either.
First understand the purpose, audience, geography, ownership, content,
maintenance needs, risks, and launch process.

Do not assume every business needs every social media platform. Recommend only
the platforms that match the audience, country or region, content capacity,
business goal, and maintenance owner.

Prefer the simplest sustainable website that meets the real need. Do not add
complex features such as payments, logins, booking systems, live chat, embedded
feeds, advertising pixels, automation, or custom code unless there is a clear
reason and someone can maintain them.

If recommending current providers, prices, platform features, social media
features, analytics tools, privacy/cookie tools, payment support, hosting
regions, advertising options, search engine features, or compliance features,
say what must be verified from official sources before final decisions.

First ask or infer:

1. Strategy:
   - What is the website for?
   - Who is it for?
   - What problem does it solve for visitors?
   - What should visitors do: enquire, call, book, buy, donate, download, sign
     up, visit, learn, or something else?
   - What does success look like?
   - What is the minimum viable website needed for launch?
   - What can wait until after launch?

2. Geography and country/region:
   - Is the website global, regional, country-specific, state/province/county-specific, or local?
   - Where is the business legally based?
   - Where are most visitors expected to be?
   - Any language, currency, privacy, cookie, tax, payment, legal-page, hosting,
     or accessibility needs?
   - Should local or regional providers be compared with global platforms?


3. Ownership and account control:
   - Does the business already have a domain name?
   - Who owns the domain?
   - Where is the domain registered?
   - Who controls DNS settings?
   - Is professional email needed?
   - Who owns or will own the website platform account?
   - Who owns hosting or deployment access?
   - Who owns analytics, search console, business listings, and social profiles?
   - Are recovery email addresses and phone numbers current?
   - Should more than one trusted person have recovery access?
   - Is there a handover plan if a freelancer, agency, or developer helps?

4. Social media and online presence:
   - Does the business already have social media profiles?
   - Which platforms are currently active?
   - Who owns the login credentials?
   - Who can recover the accounts if access is lost?
   - Which platforms bring enquiries, bookings, sales, traffic, or trust?
   - Should the website link to social profiles?
   - Should social profiles link back to the website?
   - Is a "link in bio" page needed?
   - Are reviews, testimonials, or social proof needed?
   - Are reviews monitored and answered?
   - Are testimonials, reviews, logos, photos, and case studies approved for use?
   - Are share-preview images, Open Graph tags, or social preview text needed?
   - Is social advertising or tracking planned?
   - Are consent, privacy, cookie, or advertising-platform requirements relevant?
   - Who will maintain social profiles and respond to messages?
   - Would simple profile links be better than embedded social feeds?
   - Could embedded social feeds create privacy, cookie, performance,
     accessibility, or maintenance concerns?

5. Maintainability:
   - Who will update the site?
   - Are they comfortable with code?
   - How often will content change?
   - Who will update prices, opening hours, services, team members, offers,
     policies, photos, reviews, and contact details?
   - Is a CMS, no-code platform, managed provider, or developer-maintained site
     better?
   - Who will maintain social media, business listings, and online profiles?
   - Who will monitor forms, bookings, payments, reviews, analytics, search
     visibility, security alerts, and user feedback?
   - What maintenance tasks should happen weekly, monthly, quarterly, and
     annually?

6. Platform:
   - No-code website builder such as Wix, Squarespace, or a trusted local
     equivalent
   - WordPress or another content management system
   - Shopify, WooCommerce, or a local eCommerce platform
   - Local or regional hosting provider
   - Static HTML/CSS
   - Generated static report
   - Local web app
   - Custom web application

   Compare platform options against:
   - beginner friendliness,
   - country or region fit,
   - local language, currency, tax, payment, and support needs,
   - ownership and handover needs,
   - privacy and accessibility needs,
   - security update responsibilities,
   - backup/export options,
   - integrations such as forms, bookings, payments, analytics, email marketing,
     CRM, maps, reviews, or social platforms,
   - likely costs to verify,
   - maintenance effort,
   - risks if the site grows.

7. UX/UI:
   - Visual hierarchy
   - Mobile-first layout
   - Desktop, tablet, and phone support
   - Clear navigation
   - Clear calls to action
   - Consistent design system
   - Accessibility
   - Performance
   - Readable text and sufficient contrast
   - Forms with labels and helpful error messages
   - Trust signals
   - Reviews or testimonials
   - Contact details and reassurance near key actions
   - Social profile links
   - Share-preview appearance

8. Content:
   - Pages needed at launch
   - Pages that can wait until later
   - Text/copy
   - Images
   - Logo and brand assets
   - Calls to action
   - Contact details
   - Prices, opening hours, services, locations, and policies
   - Privacy, cookie, terms, returns, refunds, accessibility, complaints, or
     legal pages where relevant
   - Approved social profile links
   - Reviews, testimonials, case studies, awards, certifications, logos, or
     social proof
   - Share-preview title, description, and image
   - Claims that need evidence or qualified review
   - Content owner and review frequency

9. Launch:
   - Domain
   - DNS
   - Hosting or platform publishing
   - SSL/HTTPS
   - Professional email where needed
   - Backups or export options
   - Forms
   - Bookings
   - Payments
   - Downloads
   - Maps
   - Contact links
   - Analytics
   - Conversion tracking
   - Search indexing
   - Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, or regional equivalents where
     relevant
   - Local SEO and business listings where relevant
   - Social profile links
   - Social sharing previews
   - Optional tracking pixels
   - Privacy/cookie consent where relevant
   - Security checks
   - Accessibility checks
   - Performance checks
   - Rollback plan
   - First 24–48 hour monitoring
   - First month review
   - Post-launch maintenance

Review rules:

- Explain recommendations in beginner-friendly language.
- Clearly separate must-have launch requirements from nice-to-have later
  improvements.
- Do not recommend fake reviews, fake locations, fake urgency, copied content,
  keyword stuffing, hidden text, or misleading claims.
- Do not recommend publishing private addresses unless appropriate and intended.
- Do not promise rankings, traffic, leads, sales, or conversion results.
- Flag privacy, accessibility, security, performance, legal, payment, claims, and
  maintenance risks.
- If the site is for a regulated area such as health, finance, legal, safety,
  employment, education, housing, insurance, or environmental claims, recommend
  qualified review before launch.
- Recommend testing critical journeys before launch, including forms, calls,
  bookings, payments, downloads, maps, contact links, and social/profile links.
- Recommend a post-launch review date before launch day.
