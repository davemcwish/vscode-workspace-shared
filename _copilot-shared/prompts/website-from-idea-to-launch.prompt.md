Help me plan a website from the initial idea through live launch.

Assume I may have little or no experience with websites, HTML, CSS, domains,
hosting, CMS platforms, accessibility, search engine optimization, analytics,
social media, online reviews, or digital marketing.

Do not start with code.
Do not assume every business needs every social media platform. Recommend only
the platforms that match the audience, country or region, content capacity,
business goal, and maintenance owner.
First ask or infer:

1. Strategy:
   - What is the website for?
   - Who is it for?
   - What should visitors do?
   - What does success look like?

2. Geography and country/region:
   - Is the website global, regional, country-specific, state/province/county-specific, or local?
   - Where is the business legally based?
   - Where are most visitors expected to be?
   - Any language, currency, privacy, cookie, tax, payment, legal-page, hosting,
     or accessibility needs?
   - Should local or regional providers be compared with global platforms?

3. Social media and online presence:
   - Does the business already have social media profiles?
   - Which platforms are currently active?
   - Which platforms bring enquiries, bookings, sales, traffic, or trust?
   - Should the website link to social profiles?
   - Should social profiles link back to the website?
   - Is a "link in bio" page needed?
   - Are reviews, testimonials, or social proof needed?
   - Are share-preview images, Open Graph tags, or social preview text needed?
   - Is social advertising or tracking planned?
   - Who will maintain social profiles and respond to messages?
   - Would simple profile links be better than embedded social feeds?
   - Could embedded social feeds create privacy, cookie, performance,
     accessibility, or maintenance concerns?

4. Maintainability:
   - Who will update the site?
   - Are they comfortable with code?
   - Is a CMS, no-code platform, managed provider, or developer-maintained site better?
   - Who will maintain social media and online profiles?

5. Platform:
   - No-code website builder such as Wix, Squarespace, or a trusted local equivalent
   - WordPress or another content management system
   - Shopify, WooCommerce, or a local eCommerce platform
   - Local or regional hosting provider
   - Static HTML/CSS
   - Generated static report
   - Local web app
   - Custom web application

6. UX/UI:
   - Visual hierarchy
   - Mobile-first layout
   - Desktop, tablet, and phone support
   - Consistent design system
   - Accessibility
   - Performance
   - Trust signals
   - Reviews or testimonials
   - Social profile links
   - Share-preview appearance

7. Content:
   - Pages
   - Text
   - Images
   - Logo
   - Calls to action
   - Contact details
   - Privacy and legal pages
   - Approved social profile links
   - Reviews, testimonials, or social proof
   - Share-preview title, description, and image

8. Launch:
   - Domain
   - Hosting
   - SSL
   - DNS
   - Analytics
   - Search indexing
   - Social profile links
   - Social sharing previews
   - Optional tracking pixels
   - Post-launch maintenance
