## Common Website Risk Categories

- Security: unauthorized access, vulnerable plugins, weak passwords, missing backups.
- Privacy/legal: unclear consent, missing privacy notice, improper tracking setup.
- Accessibility: pages or flows that users with disabilities cannot complete.
- Performance: slow pages, large images, excessive scripts, poor mobile experience.
- Content: inaccurate copy, broken links, missing ownership, stale information.
- SEO/discoverability: blocked indexing, missing metadata, poor redirects.
- Operations: unclear support process, no monitoring, missed renewals.
- Vendor/platform: lock-in, price increases, unsupported integrations.

| --- | --- |

| Likelihood | Description |
