## Common Website Assumption Areas

- Audience: who the website is for and what they need.
- Traffic: how visitors will find the site.
- Conversion: what action visitors will take and at what rate.
- Content: what pages, media, and copy are required.
- Operations: who will update the site and respond to issues.
- Cost: hosting, tools, maintenance, marketing, and vendor costs.
- Legal/privacy: consent, tracking, disclosures, accessibility, and data handling.
- Technical: platform, integrations, performance, security, and browser/device support.
